## 1. Who we are

| Name | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Be The Good** / **Be The Good UWO** | The nonprofit / club |
| **Be The Good Care** | The caregiver-burnout app (clinical settings) |

Be The Good is a student-led nonprofit from Western University. We turn everyday kindness into real support — for caregivers, for people facing hardship in our community, and for students who need someone in their corner.

We are not a glossy charity brand. We are campus-rooted, founder-led, and action-first: notes on windshields, kits for people without housing, mentorship for incoming students, and **Be The Good Care** — an app built to ease caregiver burnout in clinical settings.

**One-line:** Student-led good, made practical — on campus and beyond.

**Elevator (15 sec):** Be The Good is a Western University nonprofit that runs three things: Be The Good Care (our app for caregiver burnout), community care like food and hygiene kits for vulnerable people, and mentorship for incoming students. We’re looking for members, volunteers, and sponsors who want that work to grow.

## 4. Voice & tone

**Voice:** Warm, direct, hopeful, student-to-student. Credible without sounding institutional.

**Tone dials:**
- Friendly > formal  
- Concrete > abstract  
- Inviting > preachy  
- Proud of Western roots > exclusive / elitist  

**Do say**
- “Be the good.” / “Join the movement.”
- “Even a few smiles count.”
- “DM us to get involved.” (social) → on web: “Get involved” / “Partner with us”
- Specifics: Be The Good Care, food kits, hygiene kits, mentorship, food bank volunteering

**Don’t say**
- Tutoring / paid tutoring alternatives (explicit ask from leadership — keep mentorship broader)
- Guilt-heavy poverty language
- Overclaiming (“we solve homelessness”)
- Founder’s personal out-of-pocket funding as website copy (use for internal sponsor pitch only)

**Sample voice**
> We’re Western students who believe kindness shouldn’t stop at a caption. We build Be The Good Care for caregivers, put kits in people’s hands, and walk with incoming students so nobody has to figure it out alone.

## 5. Visual identity

### Logo
- Circular emblem: **BE / THE / GOOD** + hand-drawn heart + Western campus silhouette (UC tower)
- Variants:
  - `logoblack.png` — for light backgrounds
  - `logowhite.png` — for dark / purple backgrounds
- Prefer full circular mark in hero/nav; don’t stretch or recolor the skyline
- Optional lockup line: “Western University” when space allows (as on social posts)

### Color system (from existing IG / Canva)

| Role | Name | Approx hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Primary | Western Purple | `#4F2683` | Logo, headings, primary buttons, key UI |
| Soft | Lavender | `#C9B8E8` / `#D4C4F0` | Section fills, cards, soft panels |
| Accent | Mustard Gold | `#D4A017` / `#C9A227` | CTAs, labels, sparkles, emphasis |
| Ground | Cream | `#F7F3EC` | Page background, breathing room |
| Ink | Near-black | `#1A1224` | Body text |
| Contrast | White | `#FFFFFF` | Logo fields, light text on purple |

**Rule:** Purple carries the brand. Gold is the spark (CTA / highlight), not the base. Cream keeps things soft and student-friendly — not stark corporate white.

### Typography direction
Match the social system: collegiate serif + clean sans + occasional script accent.

| Role | Direction | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Display / logo-adjacent | Classic serif, confident | Playfair Display, Fraunces, Libre Baskerville |
| UI / body | Friendly modern sans | DM Sans, Source Sans 3, Nunito Sans |
| Accent (sparingly) | Script or soft italic for “the” / short phrases | Great Vibes / similar — **one word max** |
| Social video captions | Bold rounded sans (IG vibe) — optional on “campus culture” modules only |

### Motifs already in brand
- Repeating “BE THE GOOD” border / ticker
- Four-point sparkles (gold or purple)
- Hand-drawn heart (from logo)
- Soft rounded pills for labels (“FOUNDER”, “CO PRESIDENT”)
- Authentic campus photography (Weldon-style spaces, real students)
- Handwritten kindness notes as proof of culture

### Photography
Prefer real Be The Good moments: kindness notes, street interviews, kit packing, campus hangouts. Avoid generic stock “holding hands over globe.”

## 6. What we do (website pillars)

Present **three main streams**. Mentorship stays; **do not mention tutoring**.

### 1) Be The Good Care (product)
**Be The Good Care** — an app built with a friend to address **caregiver burnout in clinical settings**.  
**Website framing:** Innovation from students who care about people who care for others. Name it clearly on the site; pair with product screenshots / demo video when assets land in `/public`.  
**CTA:** Learn about Be The Good Care / Watch the demo / Get involved with the product team (if applicable).

### 2) Community care (direct aid)
Raising money and resources for vulnerable populations — e.g. **food and hygiene kits** for people experiencing homelessness.  
**Website framing:** Practical help, funded together (members, sponsors, campus).  
**CTA:** Donate / Sponsor a kit / Partner with us.

### 3) Mentorship (student support)
Mentorship for incoming students who may not have the same resources as peers.  
**Website framing:** Belonging, guidance, peer support — **not** academic tutoring services.  
**CTA:** Become a mentor / Request a mentor (when intake exists).

### Emerging / coming soon
- **Food bank volunteering** during the school year (Arpi’s priority)  
  Frame as: “This year we’re organizing regular food bank volunteer shifts — join a crew.”

### Culture & campus presence (supporting content, not a 4th equal pillar)
- Kindness notes (“I hope you know how loved you are”)
- Campus conversations (“Who is your biggest inspiration in life?”)
These prove the vibe: human, local, shareable. Use in About / Stories / Feed modules.

## 9. Guardrails (non-negotiables)

1. **No tutoring** language on the public website.  
2. Name consistently: **Be The Good** / **Be The Good UWO**; IG **@bethegooduwo**.  
3. Keep Western affiliation clear but student-led (not “official university department”).  
4. Don’t invent stats — use stories and programs until numbers are confirmed.  
5. Founder’s pocket funding = **internal** sponsor motivation only, not homepage guilt copy.  
6. Mentorship ≠ academic remediation; frame as belonging and guidance.
